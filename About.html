About Me

Hi! I'm Jacin M. Borre, a dedicated Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) student at Our Lady of Fatima University. Ever since I started exploring the world of technology, I’ve been passionate about understanding how things work—whether it's developing websites, solving technical problems, or engaging in IT seminars.

Outside of academics, I enjoy photography, especially capturing beautiful sunsets and nature. It’s my way of expressing creativity and seeing the beauty in everyday life. Photography helps me stay inspired, even when student life gets challenging. Balancing my passion for tech and creativity is what makes this journey exciting!

Through this personal website, I want to share my progress, projects, and moments that define who I am. Thank you for visiting!
