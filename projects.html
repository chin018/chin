My Featured Project: Chin World’s Digital Portfolio

This project is a personal website I built called "Chin World's Projects". It serves as my digital portfolio and creative space, where I share my hobbies, IT-related experiences, and growth as a student. Designed with HTML and CSS, this site includes sections about me, a photo gallery, and documentation of school activities such as seminars and collaborative events.

I created this to reflect who I am—not only as a BSIT student but also as someone who loves to capture beautiful moments, document progress, and grow both technically and creatively.
